The paperwork that stops a trade-in

A dealer’s trade-in desk sees a problem title and either passes or offers a number that assumes the worst. We work out the actual problem and price the actual car.

No title

Lost, never transferred, or still in the mail from the last sale. We work out what’s missing and get the car to where it can be sold.

Titles in transit

Rebuilt or salvage

A branded title doesn’t make the car worthless. We price it for what it is, with the inspection and history to back the number up.

Rebuilt and salvage titles

Not in your name

It might be inherited, titled to a company, or bought without the transfer ever being filed. We work the ownership chain instead of treating the car as a dead end.

Something else

How a trade-in works

You deal with one person from the first call to the handover.

  1. 1

    Tell us the car and the problem

    Year, make, model, and what’s wrong with the paperwork. Email, Signal, or the form below. The consultation is free.

  2. 2

    We review the title and the car

    We check the title history, the ownership chain, and the car’s condition, then work out what it takes to fix the paperwork and what the car is worth after that.

  3. 3

    You choose how to get out

    Trade it toward your next car or sell it outright. We lay out both options with real numbers.

  4. 4

    We clear the paperwork and close

    Transfer, paperwork, and logistics are on us. You hand over the keys once the title issue is resolved.

Fees are set per engagement. There’s no published rate card.
