FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Dallas acquisition — FAQ

Can you buy a car in Dallas if the title is in transit?

Yes. We close Dallas and DFW purchases when the title is still at a lender, a dealer, or in the mail. The car is only half the file; the paper is the other half.

What if the Dallas seller is not the name on the title?

Estate, company, and skipped-transfer cars in Highland Park, Preston Hollow, and the rest of DFW still close when the chain can be shown. We do not invent owners.

Do you source used Ferraris and Porsches for Dallas buyers?

Yes. Pre-owned exotic sourcing and new allocations. Private party, out of state, and cars that never hit a local lot. Press photos on the site are not inventory.
