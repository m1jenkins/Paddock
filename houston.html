Vehicle acquisition in Houston

Houston buyers usually have a car in mind — a 911, a 296, an M5, an Aston — and a problem attached to it: the title is in transit, the seller is not on the title, or the car is branded. Provenance buys on your side of that table.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Houston acquisition — FAQ

Can you buy a car in Houston if the title is still in transit?

Yes. Title-in-transit purchases are a regular part of the work. We keep the deal moving, track the paper, and get the title into your name instead of walking because the seller is still waiting on a lender, a dealer, or TxDMV.

What if the Houston seller’s name is not on the title?

Estate, company title, skipped transfers, and out-of-state owners are closable if the chain can be documented. We work that chain rather than treating a name mismatch as a dead deal.

Do you source pre-owned Ferraris, Porsches, and Aston Martins in Houston?

Yes. New allocations and used cars. Pre-owned exotic sourcing includes cars that are not on a Houston lot this week — private party, out of state, and complicated paper.
