Start an acquisition

Walking into a dealership and driving home the same day is not how these cars get bought.

Manufacturers allocate high-demand models to their dealers in small numbers, based on each dealer’s sales history with the brand. The dealer then decides which customers get them, and people who have bought there before usually come first. On the most wanted cars, the dealer can also add a market adjustment that runs tens of thousands over MSRP.

We use dealer relationships across Texas and elsewhere in the US to get you an allocation or a factory order, and we negotiate the price on your side of the table.

Four ways to the car

After the first conversation, we tell you which of these fits your car, your spec, and your timeline.

Factory order

You choose the spec and a dealer places the order with the factory. We go through the configurator with you first: paint, interior, performance packages, and options.

Allocation transfer

Another dealer holds an allocation for the car and is willing to release it. We find that dealer and arrange the purchase.

Dealer locate

The car is already built and sitting with a dealer somewhere in the country. We find it and negotiate the price.

Waitlist

When there’s no faster route, we put you on the right dealers’ lists and stay in touch with them until a car comes up.

Typical factory order times

Porsche factory order 3–6 months
Bentley or Rolls‑Royce custom order 6–12 months
Ferrari build slot 12–24 months

Times vary by model and spec. You get an estimate for your car at the start and updates while it’s built.
