Pre-owned exotic car sourcing in Texas

Used, specific, not a lot

Pre-owned exotic sourcing is a search for a particular used Ferrari, Aston Martin, Porsche, or similar car — not a walk down a dealer row. The F430 photographs on this page are Ferrari press images. They are not cars Provenance has for sale.

Used cars trade with private owners, out-of-state dealers, auctions, and people who never listed the car honestly. The Texas buyer still has to register the result. Title in transit and name mismatches show up here as often as they do on a Macan.

Ferrari, Aston Martin, Porsche

An F430, a 458, a 488, a used 296: the model changes the inspection list, not the job. You need service history that matches the car, a PPI by someone who works on that marque, and a title that can close in Texas. Allocation is a different path; see the Ferrari allocation guide and 296 sourcing.

Aston Martin is the same work with a thinner used market. DB11, Vantage, older GTs: spec and service matter more than a pretty listing photo. Porsche GT cars and special 911s are hunted constantly; the failures are usually price, accident history, or a seller who cannot produce paper.

What we do, and what we are not

Search to spec. Inspect. Check the title. Negotiate. Transport. Buyer-side. There is no Provenance showroom and no “our inventory.” If the car is in another state with an estate title, that is still a Texas acquisition if you are the buyer. See cars not in the seller’s name.
